01 — About

A practice built on clarity.

I’m Mohammed, a designer focused on interfaces that feel calm, precise, and easy to trust. I work across layout, typography, and visual systems, from the first sketch to a finished screen.

I care about the quiet parts of a design: spacing, contrast, and the way a page behaves on a phone as well as a desktop. The goal is work that stays elegant up close and clear at a glance.
